03How we would start

Month one is discovery. No exceptions.

If month one does not land, we do not continue, and you keep the work.

  1. Discovery and brand voice

    Category audit of the jerky and meat stick shelf, audience map, voice and visual rules, content pillars.

  2. Prelaunch feed

    Build the channels, seed the story, start the calendar, and line up early creator partners.

  3. Launch window

    A steady posting cadence, content made for TikTok first, community management, and reporting in plain numbers.

  4. Scale when ready

    Add paid social and creator programs once organic shows which messages and flavors pull.
