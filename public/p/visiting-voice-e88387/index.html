02What we treat for adults and seniors

Specialized in the adult and aging voice.

Every program begins with a thorough evaluation and ends with measurable goals, a written plan, and training for the people who care for your loved one every day.

  1. 01

    Swallowing and Dysphagia

    Bedside swallow evaluations, safe diet recommendations, and exercises that aim to reduce choking and aspiration risk.

    About swallowing care
  2. 02

    Stroke and Aphasia Recovery

    Rebuilding speech, understanding, reading, and writing after a stroke.

    About aphasia therapy
  3. 03

    Brain Injury (TBI) Recovery

    Attention, memory, word finding, speech, and swallowing support after a concussion or traumatic brain injury.

    About brain injury therapy
  4. 04

    Cognitive Communication

    Memory, attention, and problem solving strategies for dementia and mild cognitive impairment.

    About cognitive therapy
  5. 05

    Parkinson's and Voice

    Programs to strengthen a soft or strained voice and keep conversation clear and confident.

    About voice therapy
  6. 06

    Motor Speech

    Treatment for slurred or effortful speech caused by neurological conditions.

    About motor speech
  7. 07

    Caregiver Training

    Hands on coaching for families and staff on feeding safety and communication at home.

    About caregiver training

03For families

No waiting rooms. No drives across town.

We see your parent where they are most comfortable, and we keep you in the loop from the first call. Medicare Part B can cover speech therapy with a plan of care certified by your doctor, nurse practitioner, or physician assistant, and the practice's Medicare enrollment is in progress. Private pay is also available.

A Guide for Families

How it works

  1. 1

    A conversation first

    A short call to understand your loved one, their history, and what matters most to your family.

  2. 2

    An evaluation where they live

    A licensed SLP visits the home or community and completes a full assessment with a written plan.

  3. 3

    Therapy with real updates

    Consistent sessions with the same therapist whenever possible, plus regular progress notes shared with family and physicians.

04Our promise

07Meet your therapist

Whitney Albert, M.S., CCC-SLP

Since 2012, Whitney has devoted her work to two seasons of life: the school years and the later years. A graduate of Florida State University, she specializes in swallowing, where she is VitalStim certified, and in recovery after traumatic brain injury. She founded The Visiting Voice on a quiet conviction: that a second grader and a grandparent alike deserve care that is patient, personal, and given with dignity.
