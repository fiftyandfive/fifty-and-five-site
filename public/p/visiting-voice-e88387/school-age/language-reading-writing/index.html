Language for Reading and Writing

Reading and writing are built on spoken language. Literacy based language therapy works on the skills underneath: the sounds in words, vocabulary, sentence structure, and understanding and retelling what was read.

SIGNS IT MAY HELP

  • Trouble rhyming or breaking words into their sounds
  • Reading the words but not understanding what was read
  • Written work that is much weaker than your child's spoken ideas
  • Difficulty retelling a story or summarizing a lesson

WHAT A SESSION LOOKS LIKE

Sound awareness, vocabulary, and sentence work tied to real books and class assignments, with practice retelling and writing about what was read. We are glad to coordinate with your child's teacher when you agree.

All questions and answers

Does my child need speech therapy?

Consider an evaluation if your school age child is hard to understand, has trouble following directions or telling a story in order, struggles with reading and writing because of language, stutters, or finds conversations with peers difficult. An evaluation answers the question clearly and gives you a written plan either way.

Do you work with my child's school or IEP team?

Yes, with your written permission. Whitney can review an IEP or 504 plan, share evaluation results with the school, and coordinate goals so private therapy and school support reinforce each other.
