01 Families and patients

Caring for a parent, spouse, or child

Tell us what you are noticing: coughing at meals, trouble finding words, a softer voice, or new confusion. For a school age child: speech that is hard to understand, trouble with language or reading, or stuttering. We will explain your options, including Medicare, private pay, and the Step Up scholarship where eligible.

02What happens next

From first call to first visit.

No pressure and no obligation. The first conversation is simply about understanding your situation.

  1. 1

    We reach out to you

    A real conversation about your loved one, your questions, and whether our care is the right fit.

  2. 2

    We learn about your loved one

    Health history, current diet, daily routine, and what matters most, so the evaluation starts in the right place.

  3. 3

    We schedule the evaluation

    A licensed SLP visits the home or community, or sees your child at home, for a full evaluation and a written plan of care.
