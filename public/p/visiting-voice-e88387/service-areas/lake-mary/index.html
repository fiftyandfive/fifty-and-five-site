All questions and answers

Is there a speech therapist who makes home visits in Lake Mary?

Yes. The Visiting Voice is a mobile speech therapy practice that visits older adults at home, in assisted living, in memory care, and in skilled nursing in Lake Mary. School age children are seen at home after school.

Does Medicare cover in home speech therapy in Lake Mary?

Yes, when it is medically necessary. Medicare Part B covers outpatient speech therapy at home or in assisted living in Lake Mary, the patient does not need to be homebound, and a physician, nurse practitioner, or physician assistant certifies the plan of care. The usual Part B deductible and coinsurance apply. The practice's Medicare enrollment is in progress, so Medicare is not billed yet.

How much does private pay speech therapy cost in Lake Mary?

Rates are the same in Lake Mary as everywhere we serve: a comprehensive evaluation with a written report is $275, and treatment sessions are $175 for 60 minutes, $135 for 45 minutes, and $100 for 30 minutes. A package of eight 60 minute sessions is $1,280. Each private pay home visit adds a flat $25 travel fee.

Do you treat swallowing problems and brain injury in Lake Mary?

Yes. Swallowing (dysphagia) and traumatic brain injury are Whitney Albert's two specialties. She is VitalStim certified and treats adults recovering from a stroke or brain injury, and school age children returning to school after a concussion, in Lake Mary and nearby.
