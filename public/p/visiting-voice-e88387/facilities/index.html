For facilities and health systems

A dependable speech therapy partner, on your schedule.

Coverage, on site therapy, staff training, and referral follow up for senior living communities, skilled nursing, hospitals, and physician practices across Greater Orlando. Led by Whitney Albert, M.S., CCC-SLP, practicing since 2012, with a focus on older adults. Clear agreements, clean documentation, and one accountable owner.

01Partnership models

Four ways to work together.

Start with one, combine several, or change as your census does. Every arrangement is set out in a written agreement before care begins.

01
Rates by agreement
Based on coverage hours and scope

PRN and Vacancy Coverage

Licensed SLP coverage for leaves of absence, open positions, weekends, and caseload spikes in skilled nursing and rehab. Under consolidated billing your facility bills Medicare, and we invoice you as agreed. Rates by agreement, based on coverage hours and scope.

  • Evaluations, treatment, and documentation completed in your EMR
  • A single day, a few weeks, or a standing schedule
  • Whitney, and as the team grows, clinicians she trains, focused on adult and geriatric caseloads
02
Billed to residents
Assisted and independent living

On Site Therapy Days

A set day each week or month when a licensed SLP sees residents in your community. We bill private pay, or Medicare Part B once enrollment is complete, for each resident directly, with a certified plan of care on file for each Medicare resident.

  • Swallow and communication evaluations for residents who need them
  • Coordination with your nursing and wellness team
  • Family education sessions for residents' families
03
$350 per session
At your community

Staff In Services

Practical, hands on training for nurses, CNAs, dining staff, and life enrichment teams, tailored to your residents and scheduled around your shifts.

  • IDDSI diet levels and texture modification
  • Safe feeding and positioning at meals
  • Communicating with residents living with dementia
  • Recognizing signs of aspiration risk
04
Billed to the patient
Private pay, or Medicare Part B once enrollment is complete

Hospital and Physician Referral Follow Up

When a patient leaves the hospital or your clinic with speech, cognitive, or swallowing needs, we see them at home or in their community and close the loop with the referring provider.

  • Evaluation report sent to the referring provider
  • Plan of care returned for certification by the doctor, nurse practitioner, or physician assistant
  • Progress reports at least every ten visits
02How it works

From first call to first visit.

  1. 1

    Partner call

    A short call to learn your census, current gaps, and what a good therapy partner looks like for your team.

  2. 2

    Credentialing

    License verification and the credentialing documents your facility requires are provided before the first visit.

  3. 3

    Schedule set

    Coverage days, hours, rates, and point people are agreed in writing, so your staff know exactly who is coming and when.

  4. 4

    Monthly summary report

    Each month you receive a summary of visits, hours, and open recommendations. Clinical detail stays in the medical record.

03What you can expect

Commitments, in writing.

The standards below go into every partner agreement.

A named clinician

The same SLP whenever possible, introduced to your team by name.

Documentation standards

Notes written to Medicare documentation standards, with clear recommendations your nurses can act on.

Licensed in Florida

Florida licensed speech language pathologist. License verification is shared before the first visit.

A direct line to the owner

Questions and concerns go to Whitney Albert, the practice owner, not a call center. She is VitalStim certified and has practiced since 2012, with a focus on older adults.

04Referrals

Send a referral.

Hospitals, physicians, case managers, and facility teams can introduce a referral through the website form, and we will arrange a secure way to send the details. A referral fax and secure email will be published when the practice opens. We contact the patient or family to schedule and confirm receipt with you.

A complete referral includes

  • Physician order or signed referral
  • Face sheet with Medicare or insurance information
  • Diagnosis and relevant medical history
  • Current diet order and any recent swallow study
  • Contact for the patient, family, or responsible party
Referral details

The referral fax number and secure email will be published when the practice opens.

Please do not send patient information through the website form.
