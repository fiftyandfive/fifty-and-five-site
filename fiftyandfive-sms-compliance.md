## 4. Program messages

Configure these as the automatic replies in Quo. Every message starts with the
brand name, which carriers check for.

Opt-in confirmation, sent once when a contact form submission with consent
arrives:

> Fifty & Five: Thanks for reaching out. You'll get texts about your inquiry, like call scheduling and project updates. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

HELP reply:

> Fifty & Five: For help, call (321) 450-7550 or email hello@fiftyandfive.com. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

STOP reply:

> Fifty & Five: You're unsubscribed and won't receive more texts from us. Reply START to resubscribe.

START reply:

> Fifty & Five: You're resubscribed to texts about your inquiry. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Sample messages for the registration form:

> Fifty & Five: Hi Dana, this is Lucas. Thanks for your note about social for your restaurant group. Does Thursday at 2pm ET work for a 30 minute call? Reply STOP to opt out.

> Fifty & Five: Reminder, your call with Lucas is tomorrow at 10am ET. The meeting link is in your calendar invite. Reply STOP to opt out.

> Fifty & Five: Your audit is ready and in your inbox. Reply here with any questions. Reply STOP to opt out.
